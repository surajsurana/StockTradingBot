# Analysis: 52-Week High Momentum — PASS

**Mechanism and drivers.** The strategy earned +701 expectancy/trade with a modest 43.75% win rate but a strong 2.41 profit factor — a classic momentum signature: fewer winners, but winners run far longer (94.8-day avg hold, close to the full 126-day time-stop) while losers are capped by the 8% protective stop. Sortino (7.58) vastly exceeding Sharpe (0.626) confirms the return distribution is right-skewed — most of the pain is small/frequent, most of the gain is concentrated and large, consistent with a few 52-week-high breakouts running to the time-stop uninterrupted (no percentile-based early exit to trim them).

**Regime/sector dependence.** Performance was regime-skewed: bullish-entry-day trades contributed more than double bearish-entry-day trades (15,516 vs 6,922), consistent with momentum's known dependence on trend continuation. Sector P&L is highly concentrated: Healthcare (+18,223) alone exceeds total P&L (22,438), with Capital Goods (+7,811) the only other meaningfully positive contributor; eight of twelve sectors were net negative. This is a two-sector strategy wearing a market-wide label — the annual return (+26.16% in 2026 vs -3.72% in 2025) and the single blowout month (Aug 2026: +22.68%) suggest the whole track record leans on a handful of episodes, not broad-based edge.

**Follow-ups.** (1) Test sector-conditioning or exclude the eight negative sectors to see if expectancy survives outside Healthcare/Capital Goods. (2) Given single-vintage variance is flagged as the largest structural deviation, rerun with staggered/overlapping vintages to check if the Aug-2026 concentration is a single-path artifact rather than a repeatable effect.
