**Why it passed:** With 366-383 trades, a 30% win rate, and PF 1.84, this is a classic momentum profile — most trades are small losers cut by the 8% stop or the 126-day time-stop, while a minority of winners run for the full 6-month K-holding window and drive the expectancy (+2,664.9/trade overall, +1,713.65/trade OOS). The 100%-positive walk-forward windows plus the OOS holdout are the strongest evidence, since they show the edge isn't a backtest artifact of one lucky vintage — important given the single-vintage construction (vs. the paper's overlapping portfolios) inherently produces higher path variance per episode.

**Dependencies:** P&L is concentrated in Capital Goods, Healthcare, IT, and Energy/FMCG — classic cyclical/quality winners-keep-winning sectors over 2021-24 — while Auto, Telecom, and Financial Services were net negative, consistent with momentum crashing in mean-reverting or rate-sensitive names. The bullish-regime P&L (579k) roughly 1.5x the bearish-regime P&L (396k) is expected for a long-only momentum book, but the fact that bearish-day entries were still solidly profitable suggests the edge isn't purely beta-driven. Monthly returns show huge dispersion (-138% to +139%) with a brutal 2020-03 (-48%) and 2025-01 drawdown cluster, and max drawdown of 35% — the stop-loss/position-sizing overlay (absent from the source paper) is likely what kept this survivable rather than catastrophic, given no risk management exists in the original methodology.
