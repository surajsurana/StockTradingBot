**Why it passed**

The 52-week-high momentum effect shows up clearly but concentrated: with a 33% win rate and 2.13 profit factor, the strategy is a classic "cut losses, let winners run" momentum system — most trades lose small (capped near 8% by the protective stop), while a minority of breakouts run to the full 126-day time-stop and deliver outsized gains. This is consistent with expectancy of ₹2,132/trade despite only 1-in-3 trades winning.

The regime and sector data confirm this is a trending-market phenomenon: 97% of total P&L (₹645k of ₹665k) came in bullish-regime entries, and returns were essentially built in three episodes — 2021 (+110%), 2023 (+231%), 2024 (+288%) — periods of strong sectoral trends (Capital Goods +₹279k, Power +₹161k, both classic multi-quarter breakout sectors in that window). Conversely, 2020 (COVID crash, -34% in March alone) and 2025-2026 drawdown months show the strategy bleeding when new highs are false breakouts in choppy/bearish tape — Metals & Mining (-₹32k) and Services (-₹29k) look like whipsaw-prone sectors for this signal. The 35% max drawdown and single-vintage construction (per disclosed scope) mean this concentration is partly an artifact of realizing one entry path per episode rather than the paper's smoothed overlapping-vintage average — the true factor may be steadier than this run suggests.
