# Analysis: Overnight Return Anomaly (REJECT)

**Why it rejected — mechanism first.** The auditor's 0/2 walk-forward pass rate is consistent with a strategy that was never actually testing what it intended to. The disclosed execution gap is the key: because this engine can only express single entry/exit prices with next-day-open fills, the realized trade spans Open(t+1)→Open(t+2) — a full intraday session plus its flanking overnights — rather than a pure close-to-open overnight round trip. Per the source literature, that captured intraday session is expected to move *opposite* the overnight-persistence effect, so the strategy is structurally netting the signal against a same-size contaminating force. A profit factor barely above breakeven (1.08) and win rate under 50% (0.4717) is exactly the signature of an edge being partially cancelled by a contaminating return stream rather than a genuine null result.

**Dependencies.** The overall marginal profitability (CAGR 13.6%, expectancy +₹21.82/trade) is not broad-based: P&L is concentrated in Auto/Healthcare/Capital Goods/Power, while Consumer Services (-23.5k), Media (-7k) and Services (-7.3k) bled steadily — sector-idiosyncratic, not a market-wide overnight effect. It's also regime-skewed: bullish-day entries generated more than double the P&L of bearish-day entries (177k vs 80k), suggesting whatever edge exists is riding market drift, not a standalone overnight anomaly. The monthly return series is also wildly unstable (e.g. +80.8% Apr-2026 vs -42% Feb-2025), inconsistent with a small, real microstructure effect.

**Follow-up ideas.** (1) Before re-testing, build a session-aware execution model (true close-to-open fill) — this result is a contaminated lower bound, not a clean rejection of the underlying mechanism. (2) Re-run with sector exclusion/neutralization on Consumer Services and Media, and separately re-test only bullish-regime days, to see if the netted edge survives once the intraday contamination and regime dependency are stripped out.
