# Analysis: Turtle Trading System 2 (Donchian Breakout) — REJECT

**Why this outcome is consistent with the mechanism.** System 2 is a pure long-term trend-following breakout: it wins big and rarely (win_rate 26.1%, profit_factor 1.32), relying on a handful of extended trends to pay for many small losses. That pattern shows up clearly in the monthly returns — three months (2023-10 at +43.7%, 2024-02 at +35.6%, 2024-06 at +14.4%) drive nearly all the cumulative gain, sitting inside a long stretch of grinding, choppy negative months (Oct-21 through Jun-22, most of 2025). The rolling-annual series confirms this: performance rockets from -42% to +147% by Feb-2024 on the back of that trend cluster, then decays steadily back to negative territory through most of 2025 as breakouts whipsawed. This is exactly the fragility the auditor flagged: in-sample/walk-forward captured the 2023-24 trend window, but the OOS expectancy of -113.67 shows the strategy did not find comparable trends on unseen data — it needs rare, large, sustained moves that didn't recur.

**Dependencies.** Gains concentrated in Capital Goods and Healthcare (+$64.8k, +$30.0k), which alone exceed total P&L; Metals & Mining, IT, Services, Telecom were net drags (-$9.6k to -$13.3k), suggesting sector-specific trend persistence rather than a broad edge. Bearish-regime entries outperformed bullish (+$39.5k vs +$19.2k), unusual for a long-only breakout system and worth scrutiny — possibly reflecting sharp mean-reversion snaps after bearish-day breakdowns rather than genuine trend continuation.

**Follow-ups.** (1) Test regime-conditioning explicitly — did the two winning months coincide with a market-wide breadth expansion that a single-market breakout system can't reliably time again? (2) Given only one asset class here versus the original's 20+ uncorrelated futures markets, a natural next hypothesis is running System 2 across a basket of NSE sectors/indices simultaneously to see if diversification restores the edge that concentration destroyed.
