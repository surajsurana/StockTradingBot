# Analysis: Turtle Trading System 2 (Long-Only, NSE Cash Equities)

**Mechanism fit:** The 38% win rate paired with a 2.13 profit factor is textbook trend-following signature — most breakouts fail and get cut early, but the rare sustained trend (avg hold 51.7 days) pays for all the losers many times over. This is exactly how a long-term Donchian breakout is supposed to behave, and the walk-forward consistency (100% of windows positive, holdout expectancy +647.78/trade) confirms the edge isn't a curve-fit artifact.

**Dependence structure:** Performance is lumpy and concentration-driven rather than broad-based. A handful of months (2023-10: +289%, 2024-02: +213%, 2021-06: +205%) account for a disproportionate share of the 352%/299% annual returns in 2023/2024 — classic "few big trends carry the book" behavior, but it also means the strategy is exposed to regime droughts (2025-01/02 deeply negative, 31% max drawdown). Sector P&L shows the edge is concentrated in trending cyclicals — Capital Goods, Power, Financial Services, Healthcare — while IT (-99k), Realty (-40k) and Services (-32k) bled money, consistent with those sectors chopping/mean-reverting rather than trending during this period. Bullish (590k) vs bearish (572k) regime P&L is nearly balanced, suggesting the long-only breakout captured up-moves in both tapes reasonably evenly — not simply riding a market beta.

**Follow-ups:** (1) Test sector-conditional filters or exclude IT/Realty/Services to see if trimming chronic laggards improves Sharpe (currently 0.726) without hurting the tail-catching months. (2) Given only 4.19% exposure yet 31% drawdown, investigate position-sizing/vol-targeting — the single-asset-class concentration (vs. the original 20+ market design) likely explains why drawdown-per-unit-exposure is so high; diversifying entries across more independent instruments should be the next test of transferability.
