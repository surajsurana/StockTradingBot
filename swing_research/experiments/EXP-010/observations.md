# Analysis: Minervini Trend Template Filter — PASS

**Mechanism and why it passed.** The strategy's edge is a classic low-win-rate/high-payoff trend-following profile: 28.3% win rate against a 2.08 profit factor and +423 expectancy, consistent with a template that filters for structurally strong stocks (price/MA stack, 52-week high proximity) and then rides trends until a 50-day MA violation. This mechanism inherently produces long, lumpy tails rather than frequent small wins — visible in the return series, where 2021-06 (+41.9%) and 2021-08 (+59.3%) alone account for most of the CAGR. The annual return table (2018: -4.0%, 2019: -0.7%, 2020: +2.6%, 2021: +128.9%) shows the strategy was flat-to-losing for three years and then captured one explosive regime. This is exactly what a trend-template filter should do: stay quiet in chop, compound hard when a genuine bull leadership cycle emerges (2020-21 post-COVID recovery).

**Dependencies.** P&L by regime confirms this is a bull-market-dependent strategy: +123,611 in bullish-labeled entries vs. only +6,356 in bearish ones — the edge essentially doesn't exist outside uptrends, as expected for a trend template. Sector concentration is heavy: Chemicals (+42.5k), Power (+36.4k), IT (+23.3k) and Financials (+23.2k) drove nearly all profit, while Healthcare (-9.0k), Construction (-4.7k) and Realty (-3.6k) were persistent drags — likely sectors where the RS-approximation or exit rule keeps admitting/holding weak trends.

**Follow-ups.** (1) Since the RS substitute and entry-trigger proxy are flagged as the biggest fidelity gaps, rerun with a stricter VCP-pivot-breakout trigger to test whether expectancy improves (current design likely understates true SEPA). (2) Given the exit rule's undocumented status and the 32.85% max drawdown, test trailing-stop or 50-day-MA-plus-ATR variants to see if the 2020-02/03 (-9.7%/-12.8%) and 2019-10 (-15.5% rolling) drawdown clusters can be reduced without sacrificing the 2021 tail capture.
