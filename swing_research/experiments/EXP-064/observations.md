# Analysis: Minervini Trend Template Filter — PASS

**Mechanism fit.** The trend-template/VCP mechanism is built to catch large, low-frequency trend moves, and the numbers confirm this signature: a low 26.7% win rate paired with a 1.72 profit factor and +525 expectancy means the strategy is a classic "cut losses fast, let a few winners run" system. This is corroborated by the monthly returns — three months (Mar-24, Apr-24, Jul-24) contributed +141.9% combined, while most other months were flat-to-negative. The strategy is essentially a small number of huge trend-following winners subsidizing frequent small losses, exactly what a template-qualification + 50-day-MA-exit design should produce.

**Regime/sector dependency.** P&L is concentrated in bullish-regime entries (₹123.7k vs ₹47.5k bearish), consistent with a trend-continuation filter needing an uptrending tape to work. Sector P&L is dominated by Capital Goods (₹73.3k) and Financial Services (₹55.8k) — both classic 2023-24 momentum leadership groups — while Power (-₹11.5k) and Chemicals (-₹10.9k) dragged results, suggesting the RS-substitute is picking up laggard/rotational names in weaker sectors that don't sustain trend qualification. The rolling-annual chart shows the entire edge was earned in the Oct-23–Oct-24 window; 2025 was a prolonged drawdown, meaning the strategy is regime-fragile, not sector-agnostic.

**Follow-ups.** (1) Test entry lag (require N days post-qualification, or actual pivot breakout) to see if delaying entry improves the win rate given the disclosed noisy-trigger risk. (2) Isolate Capital Goods/Financials as a sector overlay filter given their outsized, consistent contribution versus Power/Chemicals.
