# Analysis: Crypto Daily Trend Timing (Faber 10-Month SMA, Daily Cadence)

**Mechanism and why it passed.** This is a long-only trend-following filter (price vs. 10-month SMA, checked daily) with no leverage and a 20% stop. The extremely low win rate (17.1%) paired with a healthy profit factor (2.06) and positive expectancy (+95.9/trade) is the classic Faber-style signature: most entries are false starts that get stopped out for small losses, but the strategy survives on a handful of massive trend captures. The monthly returns confirm this starkly — single months like 2022-01 (+582%), 2024-09 (+690%), and 2025-10 (+798%) account for essentially all the P&L, while dozens of surrounding months bleed -1% to -80%. This is a "few home runs carry the book" profile, not a consistently edge-generating system.

**Dependencies.** With only one "sector" bucket (crypto, undifferentiated) and no hour/regime breakdown, the strategy's fate is entirely dependent on capturing 2-3 major bull-market breakouts (late 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025 rallies) rather than any time-of-day or cross-asset regime effect. The 42% max drawdown and 0.44 Sharpe (vs. 1.98 Sortino) show the cost of the whipsaw months is real but tolerable only because upside outliers dwarf it.

**Follow-ups.** (1) Test sensitivity to the 20% stop placement/width, since daily cadence plus a fixed stop likely drives most of the 83% losing trades — a volatility-scaled stop may cut noise trades without dulling trend capture. (2) Decompose P&L by which specific rallies were caught vs. missed to check if edge concentrates in one or two idiosyncratic events (fragility test) rather than a repeatable timing mechanism.
