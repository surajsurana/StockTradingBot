# Analysis: Turtle System 2 (Long-Only) — PASS

**Why it passed the way it did:** The core Turtle mechanism — cut losses fast, let winners run on trend continuation — shows up clearly in the low win rate (38%) paired with a healthy profit factor (2.14) and expectancy (₹4,999/trade). This is the classic "many small losses, occasional huge wins" signature of a breakout-following system, confirmed by the monthly returns table: single months like 2023-10 (+289%), 2024-02 (+213%), 2021-06 (+205%), and 2026-07 (+184%) are doing most of the heavy lifting, while most other months are modest or negative. Sharpe (0.73) is unremarkable but Sortino (1.97) is much stronger, consistent with a strategy whose downside is many small stop-outs rather than large drawdown shocks in the tail — though the 31% max drawdown (e.g., the 2022 rolling-return collapse from +70% to -30%) shows regime risk is real.

**Dependencies:** P&L is heavily concentrated in Capital Goods, Power, and Financial Services — sectors that had sustained multi-month directional runs (capex/power cycle 2021-24) — while IT, Realty, and Services were net drags, likely from choppy/mean-reverting price action that generates repeated whipsaw losses under a breakout entry. Bullish (₹590.8k) and bearish (₹579.0k) regime P&L are almost balanced, suggesting the long-only breakout entry itself is regime-agnostic at entry — it's the *sector's* trend persistence, not the Nifty tape, that matters.

**Follow-ups:** (1) Test regime/sector filters — restrict entries to sectors with trending ADX/12-month momentum to avoid the IT/Realty bleed. (2) Since original System 2 relied on ~20 uncorrelated futures markets for smoothing, and this cash-equity single-asset-class version had only 234 trades and 4.2% exposure, test a multi-instrument NSE F&O overlay (index + liquid stock futures) to reduce the reliance on 4-5 outlier months for total return.
