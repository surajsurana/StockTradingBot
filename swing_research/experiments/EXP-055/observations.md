# Analysis: Minervini Trend Template Filter — PASS

**Mechanism and why it worked:** The trend template's core logic — filtering for stocks in confirmed structural uptrends (above rising 50/150/200-day MAs, near highs, relative strength) — naturally concentrates trades in low-frequency, high-conviction breakouts. This shows up starkly in the numbers: only a 26.27% win rate but a 1.80 profit factor and +2,139.77 expectancy, the classic trend-following signature of many small losses cut via the 50-day MA exit and a few outsized winners left to run. The extreme skew is visible in monthly returns — single months like 2024-03 (+363%) and 2024-08 (+298%) dwarf the routine -5% to -10% chop months, indicating a handful of trades did the heavy lifting. This is fully consistent with a state-transition/VCP-breakout style filter: it's designed to catch the start of large trend moves, not to win often.

**Dependencies:** The regime split (bullish $1.24M vs bearish $0.31M) confirms this is a long-only trend strategy that needs directional/bullish tape to fire — unsurprising given no short-side rules were disclosed. Sector concentration is heavy and mechanistic, not diversified alpha: Capital Goods and Financial Services alone contributed ~$1.15M of the $1.56M total P&L, while Metals & Mining, Construction, and Power were net drags (-$80.7k, -$49.3k, -$48.3k), suggesting the RS/trend criteria structurally favor sectors with sustained institutional-driven uptrends over cyclical/commodity names with choppier trends.
