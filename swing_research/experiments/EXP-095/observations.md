# Analysis: Cross-Sectional Momentum (US) — PASS

**Why it passed:** The 976.11 expectancy and 1.87 profit factor coexist with a low 32.6% win rate — classic momentum signature: frequent small losses cut by the 8% stop, offset by a minority of large trend-following winners held toward the 126-day time-stop (avg holding 85.4 days). The auditor's walk-forward (100% positive windows) and holdout (+583.92/trade on 112 trades) confirm this isn't an artifact of a few outlier months — though the annual returns (2024: +88%, 2026: +112%, 2020: -20%) show the edge is lumpy, not smooth, consistent with a single-vintage (non-overlapping) construction that realizes one entry path per episode rather than the paper's smoothed multi-vintage average. Monthly returns confirm this: single months like 2025-10 (+93%) and 2026-09 (+76%) are doing disproportionate work, meaning the "pass" partly reflects a few large winning episodes rather than uniformly repeatable monthly edge.

**Regime dependency:** P&L splits 211,433 bullish vs. 91,162 bearish on entry-day Nifty regime — momentum works in both but is ~2.3x stronger with bullish tailwinds, as expected for a long-only trend strategy; the 2020-03 (-31.8%) and 2025-03 (-19.7%) drawdowns coincide with regime reversals, the known weak spot for momentum (crash-reversal risk), partially cushioned here by the stop-loss absent in the source paper.

**Follow-ups:** (1) Test a skip-period (e.g., 1-month gap between formation and holding) to isolate whether current understatement of momentum is real, given no-skip is flagged as a moderate gap. (2) Overlay multi-vintage/overlapping portfolios to reduce the variance concentration visible in the outlier months and test whether the "pass" survives smoothing.
