# Analysis: Minervini Trend Template Filter — PASS

**Mechanism and why the numbers look this way:** A low win rate (34%) paired with a strong profit factor (2.70) and high expectancy (₹1,313.67) is exactly the signature the Trend Template mechanism should produce: it filters for stocks already in confirmed structural uptrends (price > 150/200 MA, MA sequencing, RS strength), then rides them until a 50-day MA close-below triggers exit. Most entries will chop or fail early (hence low win rate), but the survivors run — the 50-day exit lets winners compound over ~36-day average holds. The annual/monthly returns confirm this is a trend-capture strategy, not a high-frequency edge: 2024 (+178%) and 2026 (+134%) are carried by a handful of explosive months (Apr-2024 +50%, Jul-2026 +38%, Aug-2026 +61%), while 2025 (-8.5%) shows the strategy bleeding in a chopping/rotating tape — consistent with trend-following whipsaw, not a broken filter.

**Dependencies:** P&L is heavily concentrated in Capital Goods, Financial Services, and Healthcare (~₹249k of ₹340k total), i.e., sectors that had sustained multi-month trends in this window; Chemicals (-₹16.7k) and Consumer Services (-₹5k) dragged performance, likely sectors with false breakouts or trendless ranges. The bullish-regime P&L (₹220k) roughly doubles bearish-regime P&L (₹120k), suggesting the filter is regime-tilted but not regime-dependent — it still found tradeable uptrends even on bearish Nifty days, which is a point in favor of the mechanism's robustness rather than pure beta-riding.

**Caveats on fidelity:** Given the disclosed gaps, this PASS likely *understates* true SEPA performance — no pyramiding into winners (one-directional understatement) and a raw qualification-day entry (noisier/earlier than a true VCP pivot breakout) both cut against the strategy, not for it. The RS proxy is the biggest unknown: it could be admitting a different, possibly weaker, symbol set than IBD's real screen, so sector concentration (e.g., Chemicals losses) might partly reflect RS misclassification rather than the Trend Template itself failing.

**Follow-ups:** (1) Test a real VCP pivot-breakout trigger (tightening volatility + volume contraction before breakout) instead of the raw qualification-day proxy, to see if win rate improves without sacrificing the tail-capture behavior. (2) Isolate Chemicals/Consumer Services/Telecom trades under the current filter to check whether losses stem from RS-formula false positives (i.e., stocks passing the open-source RS blend but failing IBD's true relative-strength rank) — this would help separate "filter is wrong" from "RS proxy is wrong."
