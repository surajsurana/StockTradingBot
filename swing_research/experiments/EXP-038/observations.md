**Why it passed:** The strategy clears the bar on aggregate stats (PF 1.93, expectancy +₹878/trade, 100% positive walk-forward windows, OOS holdout +₹279/trade), but the win rate is only 32%, meaning this is a classic momentum profile — most trades are small losers or time-stopped out, and a minority of large winners (likely names that kept trending for the full 126-day hold) carry the P&L. The Sortino (2.57) well above Sharpe (0.93) confirms this: downside deviation is controlled (helped by the 8% protective stop, which isn't in the original paper but is doing real risk-management work here), while upside is fat-tailed.

**Regime/sector dependence:** Performance is heavily regime-conditional — ₹82k of ₹101k total P&L (81%) came on bullish-Nifty entry days, with bearish days contributing a much thinner ₹19k. This is consistent with the mechanism: single-vintage, no-skip momentum entries need a trending tape to avoid immediate reversal. Sector concentration is stark too: Capital Goods, Healthcare, and Consumer Durables alone contribute ~₹69k, while Oil & Gas, FMCG, and Construction Materials are net drags (-₹6.7k, -₹5.7k, -₹1.7k) — defensive/cyclical-lag sectors don't carry momentum well here. The 2024 annual return (+85%) versus 2025 (-23%) shows the single-vintage construction's higher path-dependency versus the paper's smoothed overlapping portfolios, exactly as flagged.
