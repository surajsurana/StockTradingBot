# Analysis: Minervini Trend Template Filter — PASS

**Why it likely worked:** The mechanism is a low-frequency, low-win-rate/high-payoff trend-following filter — 149 trades, 28.2% win rate, but a 1.91 profit factor and +₹600 expectancy, consistent with cutting losers early via the 50-day MA exit while letting a minority of trades run (avg holding 30.5 days, only 6.28% exposure). This is exactly the shape you'd expect from a template that screens for structurally strong stocks (price>MAs, MA alignment, RS strength) rather than timing precision — most "qualifying" names fail quickly, but the survivors trend hard, which is visible in the lumpy monthly returns (+27.9%, +30.1%, +19.5% months driving the whole curve) and the outsized concentration in two annual windows.

**Dependencies:** Performance is heavily regime- and sector-dependent. Bullish-regime entries generated ~2.4x the P&L of bearish-regime entries (₹63.2k vs ₹26.3k), consistent with a trend-template being a momentum-continuation tool, not a mean-reversion one. Sector P&L is concentrated in Metals & Mining, Chemicals, and Capital Goods (~₹68k combined, >75% of total profit), while Consumer Durables and Services were net drags — suggesting the RS-approximation is picking up cyclical/commodity leadership specific to the 2021-22 window rather than a broad-based edge.

**Follow-ups:** (1) Test sector-neutralized or sector-capped position sizing to see if the edge survives outside the Metals/Chemicals leadership regime. (2) Since RS substitute and entry-trigger fidelity are the two flagged material gaps, rerun with an actual pivot-breakout trigger and IBD-style RS ranking before treating this PASS as validating real SEPA.
