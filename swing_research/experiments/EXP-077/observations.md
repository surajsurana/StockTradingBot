**Regime and sector dependence.** The edge is heavily bullish-regime dependent: bullish-day entries generated $3,014 of the $3,918 total P&L (77%), with bearish days contributing only $904 — consistent with an overnight-drift effect that is stronger or more reliable when broader market sentiment is already positive.
