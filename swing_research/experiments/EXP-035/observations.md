# Analysis: Minervini Trend Template Filter — PASS

**Mechanism and why it passed:** The strategy's low win rate (28.3%) paired with a healthy profit factor (1.72) and positive expectancy (₹277.65/trade) is the classic trend-following signature — this is consistent with the mechanism: a Template-qualification proxy selects stocks already in structural uptrends, and the 50-day MA exit lets winners run (avg hold 28.8 days) while cutting losers early via the 8% stop. The 100% positive-expectancy walk-forward windows and strong OOS holdout (+325.63/trade, 70 trades) suggest this asymmetric payoff structure is robust across time, not curve-fit to one regime.

**Dependencies:** Performance is heavily regime-dependent — bullish-day entries generated ₹51,042 vs. only ₹9,765 in bearish entries, confirming the filter needs trend confirmation to work, consistent with Minervini's stage-2 thesis. Sector concentration is stark: Healthcare, Financial Services, Power, and Capital Goods drove ~₹83k of gains, while Chemicals (-₹6,930), Automobile (-₹8,142), and IT (-₹3,769) were net drags — the Template + RS-proxy combination is clearly biased toward specific sector momentum regimes rather than being universally applicable. 2025 was a losing year (-12.07%) with a severe Jan 2025 drawdown (-14.58%), while 2024 and 2026 were strongly positive, showing real regime sensitivity beneath the aggregate pass.

**Follow-ups:** (1) Test sector-conditioned position sizing or exclusion of Auto/Chemicals/IT given the RS-proxy fidelity gap may be misclassifying leadership there. (2) Given no-pyramiding is disclosed as one-directionally understating returns, a future hypothesis should test add-to-winner sizing specifically in the bullish-regime/Healthcare-Financials-Power subset to see if true SEPA edge is larger than measured here.
