**Why it likely passed**

The mechanism here is long-only, unlevered exposure to low-beta NSE stocks — not the paper's beta-neutral, leverage-scaled BAB spread. That distinction matters for interpreting the return profile: this is really a "do quiet stocks do fine" test, and the results (48% win rate, PF 1.35, expectancy +₹175.54) show modest, grindy edge consistent with a long-only equity-selection tilt rather than a genuine market-neutral arbitrage. The regime split confirms this directly: ₹69,873 of the ₹70,566 total P&L (99%) came in bullish-Nifty entries, with bearish entries contributing almost nothing (₹693). A long-only low-beta book with no short leg simply can't monetize down-markets or the high-beta underperformance the paper's spread depends on — it's a beta-fed strategy dressed as a beta-hedged one, so its performance is structurally tied to the broad tape being up.

Sector concentration reinforces this: Auto/Auto Components (₹22.4k), Healthcare (₹16.4k), Consumer Services (₹11.9k) and Consumer Durables (₹10.8k) drove nearly all profit, while FMCG (-₹5.6k), Chemicals, Construction Materials and IT were net drags — likely defensive/low-beta names that underperformed the rally rather than protecting capital, since there's no short leg to harvest their relative weakness. The 2024 annual return (+42%) versus flat 2025-2026 also tracks broad-market strength, not factor-specific alpha.
