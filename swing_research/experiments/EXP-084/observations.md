The PASS verdict rests on very thin, lumpy statistics: 38-44 trades, a 36.8% win rate, and a payoff structure entirely dependent on a handful of outsized winners (Jan-2022 +391%, Feb-2025 +598%, Nov-2025 +96%) offsetting a long tail of small-to-moderate losers (2023-05/06/09, 2025-01/10 all near -28% to -30%). This is exactly the signature the Faber 10-month SMA mechanism should produce: a trend-following filter that gets whipsawed in choppy/declining regimes (2020, 2023, 2024) but captures the full magnitude of the 2021-22 and 2025 crypto bull legs once it's long. The 30.4% CAGR against a 0.483 Sharpe and 29.1% max drawdown confirms the return is concentrated, not smooth — recovery factor 3.11 says the big winners did eventually repair the drawdown, but the ride was rough.

There's no sector or regime breakdown (all P&L bucketed "Unknown," no entry-hour/regime data) because this is a 5-asset crypto sleeve, not equity-sector-diversified — so the "dependency" here is asset-concentration risk (5 majors) and bull-phase timing, not sector rotation. The disclosed 20% stop is a one-directional drag: it can flush a coin during a mid-cycle dip that the original monthly-close SMA rule would have tolerated, likely trimming some of the smaller trend trades before they matured, which may partly explain the low win rate despite high profit factor.
