**Why REJECT is the right call given the numbers**

The Moving Average Pullback edge is thin and fragile even in aggregate (PF 1.05, expectancy ₹34.7, Sharpe 0.31), and it fails the walk-forward test outright: 0/2 windows profitable means the positive overall PnL is being carried by a subset of history rather than a repeatable mechanism — the same failure mode flagged in SEED-ORB-2. The annual split confirms this: 2025 was net -7.75% while 2026 was +13.19%, and monthly returns swing violently (-11.8% Feb-25 to +15.0% Apr-26) with no visible regime consistency. Rolling annual returns show long stretches deeply negative (down to -4.36% into Jan-26) followed by a late-sample recovery — exactly the pattern of a strategy riding one favorable stretch, not a stable pullback edge.

The regime breakdown reinforces the mechanism story: PnL is almost entirely bullish-day dependent (+₹9,114 bullish vs -₹1,446 bearish), consistent with a trend-continuation pattern (pullback-in-uptrend) that only works when the tape cooperates — it has no edge, and likely negative edge, in adverse tape. Sector P&L is concentrated in FMCG/Metals/Services/Capital Goods while Financial Services, Consumer Services, and Telecom bled money, suggesting the moving-average pullback signal is not robust across sector microstructure/volatility regimes, just a few compatible ones.
