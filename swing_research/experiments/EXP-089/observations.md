# Analysis: Crypto Weekly Trend Timing (Faber 10-month SMA)

The verdict rests on a low win-rate (26.9%), high-payoff structure typical of trend-following: 73% of trades are small losses (whipsaws around the 10-month SMA), offset by a handful of enormous winners. This is confirmed by the monthly returns — three isolated months (2022-01: +440%, 2024-09: +454%, 2025-10: +595%) account for the bulk of the entire 1064% return-on-capital, while dozens of other months bleed -3% to -30%. Profit factor of 2.30 and expectancy of +158.87 are believable given this shape, but they are structurally dependent on the strategy being *in position* for a small number of massive crypto regime shifts (e.g., the 2021 bull run, the 2024 halving rally, and the 2025 melt-up). The 41.9% max drawdown and Sharpe of only 0.52 vs Sortino 1.19 confirm the return stream is lumpy and asymmetric, not smooth — consistent with a rules-based trend filter on a single volatile asset class rather than a diversified equity book (no sector or entry-hour breakdown exists because this is a single-instrument crypto series, not an NSE cross-sectional strategy).

Because the only change from SW-020 is monthly→weekly rebalancing, and that shift is flagged as directionally unknown, the PASS here says nothing about whether weekly cadence helped or hurt vs. monthly — that comparison is the natural next test: run SW-020 and this variant side-by-side on identical trade dates to isolate cadence effect on whipsaw frequency and slippage. Second, given three months drive nearly all P&L, a regime-conditioning follow-up (e.g., realized-vol filter before entry) could test whether losing trades cluster in specific volatility regimes.
