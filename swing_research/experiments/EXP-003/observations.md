# Performance Analysis: Turtle Trading System 2 (Long-Only, NSE Cash Equities)

The mechanism -- entering on 55-day Donchian breakouts and riding trends with wide stops -- is a low-frequency, low-hit-rate design that survives only if a small number of large winners overwhelm frequent small losses. The numbers confirm this exactly: a 41.67% win rate paired with a 2.80 profit factor and +₹2,967.74 expectancy means the system is win-*size* dependent, not win-*rate* dependent, consistent with classic trend-following. The 51.1-day average hold and 7.2% exposure show capital sits in cash most of the time waiting for the rare breakout that actually trends.

Performance is heavily regime- and concentration-dependent. 81% of the strategy's edge (₹310k of ₹356k P&L) came from bullish-regime entries, and one month -- June 2021 (+204.8%) -- and one sector -- Power (₹204k, more than the entire net P&L) -- effectively carried the result; Auto/Auto Components added another ₹94k. Meanwhile Metals & Mining (-₹17k) and IT (-₹10k) were persistent bleeders, and 2016 and parts of 2019 were flat-to-negative, showing the system needs a genuine sustained trend regime to pay for its whipsaw losses elsewhere -- it is not sector-agnostic in practice despite being sector-agnostic by design.

Given this is a single-market (NSE cash) test of a strategy built for ~20+ low-correlated futures markets, the natural follow-up is to test whether the edge is breakout-mechanism-driven or Power/2021-bull-market-driven: rerun with Power and the 2021 window excluded, and separately test the same signal across a basket of uncorrelated NSE sectors/indices to see if expectancy holds without concentration risk.
